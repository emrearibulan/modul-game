# Design Bible

## Design Authority

The design bible interprets the North Star into product design rules. If this document conflicts with [MODUL_NORTH_STAR.md](MODUL_NORTH_STAR.md), the North Star wins.

## Core Fantasy

DESIGN — ChatGPT: The player is a spacecraft survivor-engineer who physically authors a ship from earned modules, then lives with the consequences of placement, damage, and loss.

## Construction Rules

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Every major module must visibly alter the ship.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Modules should occupy physical space rather than existing only as inventory icons or stat entries.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Attachment should communicate valid positions, invalid positions, orientation, and consequences.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Ship silhouette should express the player's history and strategy.
- BLOCKED: Exact grid, socket, freeform placement, or hybrid construction model.

## Module Rules

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Modules should have visible identity, mechanical function, attachment constraints, and damage states.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Module value should depend partly on where and how it is attached.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Module synergies should be readable through spatial relationships.
- BLOCKED: Initial module taxonomy and minimum viable module count.

## Encounter Rules

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Encounters should test the current ship rather than generic player stats.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Encounter rewards should feed directly into physical construction.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: The next encounter should create risk for recently attached or exposed modules.
- BLOCKED: First encounter format, enemy behavior, objective type, and pacing.

## Damage And Loss Rules

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Damage should be visible on the module and meaningful in play.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Loss should feel like a consequence of physical exposure, poor routing, or risky tradeoffs, not random punishment.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Damaged ships should become interesting problems, not dead runs by default.
- BLOCKED: Repair economy, severing rules, salvage behavior, and permanence.

## Interaction Rules

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Placement should create at least one MVP interaction.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Orientation should eventually matter for arcs, thrust, shielding, routing, or exposure.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Power routing should eventually matter, but only after readable MVP construction is proven.
- BLOCKED: Whether power routing appears in the critical MVP or later systems proof.

## Visual Polish Direction

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Pinmoney is the graphical polish reference.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Polish should support readability, tactility, and confidence.
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Visual feedback must make attachment, activation, damage, and loss immediately legible.
- BLOCKED: Final art style, camera perspective, rendering pipeline, and asset production plan.

## Design QA Questions

- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Can the player tell what changed after attaching a module?
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Can the player explain why placement mattered?
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Can the player predict which modules are exposed or protected?
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Does damage create a compelling adaptation problem?
- DESIGN — ChatGPT: Does the ship feel authored by the player?
